Branded dark ships, so every cell of the grid applies. The card's art gains a dark drawing: the gradient ring stays as it is, the card sits on it as the dark variant's frosted ink surface so the ring's orange and blue glow through, and a soft off-white haze rises from the bottom edge where the design lays its blurred blob. A `zd:dark:` variant, declared beside `zd:plain:` in `tokens.css` and following the OS scheme under `system`, picks the drawing. The branded dark values key on the mode alone, so a host that sets only `data-zd-mode` on `<html>` gets them.
