The dark themes. The token table states every dark value, so `plain-dark` and `branded-dark` (and `mode: 'system'` under a dark colour scheme) now apply. The plain brand drops the decorative layers: the gradient border ring, the card glow, the sign-up blob and the sheet gradient give way to a flat frosted surface with a hairline border, keyed on `data-zd-brand` like the colours.
